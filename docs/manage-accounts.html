    Manage accounts

    Your own account, your customers' accounts, and the customers themselves.

    Your business account

    Your own balance, transaction history, merchant onboarding and cash dispensing.

    Subsidiary accounts

    Paga accounts for your customers, held on your behalf. Issue, hold and move money on their account.

    Persistent payment accounts

    A standing instruction that lets a customer pay you repeatedly from the same account.

    Customer registration and KYC

    Open a Paga account for a customer, with identity documents if you need them.
