    Customer registration and KYC

    Open a Paga account for a customer, with identity documents if you need them.

    Registering a customer opens a Paga account for them on your business account. The KYC variants upload photographs as well.

    On this page

    • Register a customer
    • Register a customer with KYC
    • Upload a customer account photo
    • Upload a customer identification

    Operations

    Register a customer

    #
    Business API POST/registerCustomer principal / credentials headers referenceNumber + customerPhoneNumber + customerFirstName + customerLastName

    Opens a Paga account for a customer on your business account.

    • The phone number must not already belong to a registered customer.

    Rust

    register_customer

    let customer = client
    .register_customer(RegisterCustomer::new(
        "REF-000010",
        "08064343187",
        "Bello",
        "Ramon",
        "customer@example.com",
        "1989-10-02",
    ))
    .await?;

    Register a customer with KYC

    #
    Business API POST/registerCustomerWithKYC principal / credentials headers referenceNumber + customerPhoneNumber + customerFirstName + customerLastName

    Opens a Paga account for a customer and uploads their identification photographs.

    • Sent as multipart/form-data: a customer JSON part, plus customerAccountPhoto and customerIdPhoto image parts.
    • Each photograph must be a JPEG of at most 500 KB.

    Rust

    register_customer_with_kyc

    let photo = std::fs::read("account-photo.jpg")?;

let customer = client
    .register_customer_with_kyc(RegisterCustomerWithKyc::new(
        "REF-000011",
        "08064343187",
        "customer@example.com",
        "Bello",
        "Ramon",
        "1989-10-02",
        "MALE",
        photo.clone(),
        photo,
    ))
    .await?;

    Upload a customer account photo

    #
    Business API POST/registerCustomerAccountPhoto principal / credentials headers referenceNumber + customerPhoneNumber

    Uploads a customer's account photograph.

    • Sent as multipart/form-data: a customer JSON part plus a customerAccountPhoto image part.
    • The photograph must be a JPEG of at most 500 KB.

    Rust

    register_customer_account_photo

    let photo = std::fs::read("account-photo.jpg")?;

client
    .register_customer_account_photo(RegisterCustomerAccountPhoto::new(
        "REF-000012",
        "08064343187",
        photo,
    ))
    .await?;

    Upload a customer identification

    #
    Business API POST/registerCustomerIdentification principal / credentials headers referenceNumber + customerPhoneNumber + customerIdType + customerIdNumber + customerIdExpirationDate

    Uploads a customer's identification and its photograph.

    • Sent as multipart/form-data: a customer JSON part plus a customerIdPhoto image part.
    • customerIdExpirationDate is formatted as YYYY-MM-DD.

    Rust

    register_customer_identification

    let photo = std::fs::read("id-photo.jpg")?;

client
    .register_customer_identification(RegisterCustomerIdentification::new(
        "REF-000013",
        "08064343187",
        "NATIONAL_ID",
        "12345678901",
        "2030-01-01",
        photo,
    ))
    .await?;
